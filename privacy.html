How our Discord bot handles your information

Last Updated: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot handles information.

Information We Collect

This bot does not collect, store, process, or share personal information.

The bot does not:

  • Store user IDs.
  • Store usernames.
  • Store messages.
  • Store server information.
  • Store IP addresses.
  • Store command history.
  • Store personal data of any kind.

How the Bot Functions

The bot operates solely to:

  • Join Discord servers when invited.
  • Execute commands requested by users.

The bot only processes information temporarily as required to respond to commands and does not retain that information after the command has completed.

The bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's own privacy practices and policies.

Children's Privacy

The bot does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data Security

Since the bot does not store user data, there is no retained personal information to secure or manage.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
